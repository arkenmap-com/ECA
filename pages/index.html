Wildfire simulation maps

Interactive outputs from Cell2Fire planning scenarios using observed Smallwood weather, Canadian FBP [Fire Behaviour Prediction] fuel types, and terrain-derived slope and aspect.

Planning and research only. These scenarios are conditional model experiments, not wildfire forecasts or operational decision products.

30 m fuel and terrain · 48-hour central ignition

The newest single-point scenario, using the native 30 m fuel product and the MRDEM [Multi-Resolution Digital Elevation Model] DTM [Digital Terrain Model]. Fire growth is shown in 12-hour intervals.
